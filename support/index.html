Help for AtStation: Fun While You Wait
Need help? Email leonitdev@gmail.com. Tell us your device and what happened, and we will get back to you, usually within two working days.

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to drop the moving block onto the tower. Anything hanging over the edge is cut away, while a perfect drop keeps your block at full width. Reach the stack goal to arrive at the next station. Finish all four stations in a city to collect its passport stamp.

What is free and what do I pay for?

London, with all four of its stations, is free to play and replay. Every city after London is unlocked with one one-time purchase. There are no ads and no subscription.

I bought the full journey. How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings → Restore purchase in the app while signed in to the same Apple ID (or Google account) you used to buy it. The paywall also has a Restore purchase button.

My purchase did not go through

Some payments take a moment to complete. Wait a minute, then use Restore purchase. If you were charged and the journey is still locked, email us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play order history.

How do I start over?

In Settings, use Reset journey to clear stations and stamps, or Reset stats to clear totals and personal bests. Your purchase is not affected.

Sound, vibration and motion

Sound, haptics and reduce motion can each be switched on or off in Settings.

Privacy

AtStation collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
