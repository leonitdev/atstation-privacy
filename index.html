AtStation

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026
The short version: AtStation does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

AtStation ("the app") is a casual stacking game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, and it does not send your data to us or to any third party. Specifically, the app does not use:

  • analytics or crash-reporting services
  • advertising networks or advertising identifiers
  • location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • tracking across other apps or websites

Game progress stored on your device

To remember your game, the app saves your progress locally on your device: stations and cities reached, passport stamps, best scores, game statistics, your settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion), whether you have seen the intro, and whether you have unlocked the full journey. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with "Reset journey" and "Reset stats" in Settings, or by deleting the app.

In-app purchase

The full journey can be unlocked with a one-time purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, email address, or payment details. The app only asks the store whether the unlock has been purchased, so it can enable it and restore it on your devices.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children

AtStation is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.
